# Research problems, AI methods, and digital-twin applications

Use the [dataset catalog](../metadata/dataset_catalog.csv) to select the exact files for a study. This guide covers all seven released dataset families. The [research methods](RESEARCH_METHODS.md) explain split design and joins. The [evaluation suite](../evaluation/README.md) provides scoring for the connected retrieval task and agent fixtures.

| Dataset and research problem | Example use case | Suitable AI methods and baseline | Evaluation and evidence requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Inventory](../data/inventory/): incomplete asset visibility | Identify which missing attributes most affect a segmentation review | Active learning or Bayesian uncertainty estimation; compare with random and fixed-priority inspection | Ranking stability and improvement per inspection. Withhold known attributes for a pilot and obtain independently reviewed topology for deployment claims. |
| [Inventory and graph](../data/knowledge_graph/): evidence retrieval | Produce a cited answer about source-linked CVEs or abstain when evidence is missing | Graph-RAG, embedding retrieval, or an LLM with read-only evidence; compare against exact lookup and always abstain | Exact evidence match, unsupported assertions and abstention on the 94 asset cases. Verify applicability separately using reviewed asset configurations. |
| [Knowledge graph](../data/knowledge_graph/): relationship quality | Flag unsupported candidate relationships for review | Graph embeddings, graph neural networks, or constraint checking; compare with frequency and schema rules | Precision on reviewed links and held-out relation types. Remove target relations and inverse duplicates from model inputs. A missing edge is an unknown relationship. |
| [CTI benchmarks](../data/benchmarks/): threat intelligence classification | Extract techniques or map vulnerability descriptions to weakness categories | Supervised classification, calibrated LLM classification, or retrieval augmentation; compare with lexical and majority baselines | Accuracy, macro-F1, and error at fixed coverage by task. Audit overlap with public upstream examples and apply the benchmark license. |
| [Training/query pairs](../data/training/): schema-aware query generation | Produce or repair a read-only graph query from an analyst question | Constrained LLM decoding or supervised adaptation; compare with templates | Schema validity, answer equivalence, and engine execution success. Group derived variants by source and expand the 66-query subset with independent cases for generalization studies. |
| [Synthetic records](../data/synthetic/): shortcut learning | Determine whether a detector uses incidental identifiers or generation artifacts | Supervised classification, feature attribution, or invariant representation learning; compare with shallow models and identifier-only inputs | Sensitivity to feature removal and performance across independent generation conditions. External measured traffic is needed for operational claims. |
| [Operational traces](../data/operational/): event timing and observation quality | Detect departures in event timing or distinguish introduced missing records from ordinary sequence variability | Change-point detection, self-supervised sequence learning, or probabilistic forecasting; compare with persistence and event frequency | False alerts per trace, detection delay, and transfer to held-out traces. Introduced observation defects can be labeled by construction. Physical fault and attack labels require additional data. |
| [Operational traces](../data/operational/): monitoring under a limited inspection budget | Select events for human review at a fixed budget | Active sampling or uncertainty ranking; compare with periodic and random sampling | Performance as a function of review budget, missed events, and latency. Report trace-level results and the exact observation target. |
| [Agent scenarios](../data/agent_scenarios/): policy adherence and consistency | Check whether an assistant halts or requests review when evidence or approval is missing | Rules, constrained LLM outputs, or an offline planner–verifier; compare with deterministic policy and always stop | Decision agreement, inappropriate continuation, unnecessary stops, and decision changes under reviewed paraphrases. Obtain independent cases and reviewed context for semantic checks. |

## Applying the datasets to digital twins

| Twin capability | Released inputs and role | Verification needed |
|---|---|---|
| Asset and communication view | Inventory and flow tables establish the initial asset model. Product IDs provide verified joins into the knowledge graph. | Review topology and equipment identifiers for the intended testbed. Exact key matches do not verify current communication. |
| Process and controller models | [Native examples](../examples/digital_twins/README.md) illustrate Modelica dynamics, Structured Text control, SCXML transitions, and CSP_M properties. | Execute each applicable native model. Confirm timing and state semantics against independent reference observations. |
| Observation replay and fidelity monitoring | Operational traces support event replay and monitoring of timestamp gaps and sequence variation. | Obtain signal-to-equipment mappings, units, synchronized clocks, and process-state labels to calibrate or validate a specific physical twin. |
| Cybersecurity context and advisory decisions | Knowledge graph and CTI support evidence retrieval. Agent fixtures support offline evaluation of approval, stop, and review decisions. | Establish reviewed scenario-to-equipment mappings and validate advisory outcomes. For defensive response studies, validate modeled action effects and process constraints. |

A practical end-to-end study is **evidence-grounded advisory monitoring**. Build the asset view from inventory, replay the native model and its reference cases, and provide an assistant with read-only graph evidence and explicit stop or review rules. Compare deterministic rules, an assistant using graph evidence, and an assistant also receiving model state. Measure evidence accuracy, appropriate abstention, policy adherence, and decision latency. Use reviewed state mappings before combining operational observations with the model. For a specific physical testbed, add independent runs and report calibration and validation separately.
